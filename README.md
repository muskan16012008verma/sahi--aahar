# Sahi Aahar - Smart Anganwadi Nutrition & Health Tracking System

## Overview
A technology-driven platform designed to streamline beneficiary tracking, growth monitoring, and nutritional supplement distribution in rural Anganwadis, aligning with UN SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) and UN SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being).

## Features
- Automated growth monitoring and health parameter tracking (Height, Weight, SAM/MAM identification).
- Digital health records for pregnant women, lactating mothers, and children.
- Inventory management for nutritional supplements.
- Instant alert notifications for vaccination schedules.
